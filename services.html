Mechanical Engineering Works

Our experienced, highly qualified team of mechanical engineers offer the following services:

HVAC installation, maintenance and repairs

We provide professional heating, ventilation, and air conditioning (HVAC) services, including system design, installation, preventive maintenance, troubleshooting, and repairs. We service air conditioning units, chillers, AHUs, ventilation systems, and related equipment to ensure optimal performance, indoor comfort, and energy efficiency.

Plumbing design, installation and maintenance

We offer comprehensive plumbing solutions, from system design and pipework installation to routine maintenance, leak detection, blockage clearance, and repairs. Our services cover water supply, drainage, sanitary fittings, and wastewater systems, ensuring reliable water flow, hygiene, and efficient operation.

Water pump installation, servicing and repairs

We specialize in the installation, inspection, servicing, and repair of water pumps and pumping systems. Our services include fault diagnosis, motor checks, seal replacement, performance testing, and preventive maintenance to ensure dependable water supply and efficient pump operation.

Fuel pump installation, maintenance and repairs

We provide installation, servicing, troubleshooting, and repair of fuel pumps and associated pumping equipment. We prioritize safe fuel handling, leak prevention, fire-risk control, and compliance with applicable safety standards to support reliable operations in commercial and industrial facilities.

Generator sizing, installation, servicing and repairs

We provide generator sizing based on electrical load requirements, starting currents, and anticipated demand. Our services include site assessment, installation, electrical connections, commissioning, routine servicing, fault diagnosis, and repairs to ensure reliable backup power, safe operation, and dependable performance when the main power supply fails.

Mechanical equipment repairs and preventive maintenance

We provide inspection, troubleshooting, servicing, and repair of mechanical equipment and building services systems. Through scheduled preventive maintenance and timely corrective repairs, we help reduce equipment downtime, improve operational efficiency, and extend equipment lifespan.
