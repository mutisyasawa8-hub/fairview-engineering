About Fairview Power Controls and Engineering Ltd

Fairview Power Controls and Engineering Ltd is a multidisciplinary engineering company providing reliable and innovative solutions in electrical, mechanical, and civil engineering. We specialize in electrical installations and power distribution, control panels and automation, solar power systems, HVAC installation and maintenance, plumbing, mechanical equipment servicing, building construction, structural works, and general civil engineering projects.

Our approach combines technical expertise, quality workmanship, safety, and efficiency to deliver solutions tailored to the needs of residential, commercial, and industrial clients. From designing and installing electrical systems to maintaining mechanical equipment and executing civil construction projects such as buildings and dams, we are committed to providing dependable, cost-effective, and sustainable engineering services.

At Fairview Power Controls and Engineering Ltd, we strive to build lasting partnerships by delivering projects that meet industry standards and exceed client expectations.
